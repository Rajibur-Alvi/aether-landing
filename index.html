Frequently Asked

Everything you need to know before you launch.

What is entropy scoring? ▼

Entropy scoring measures the disorder, unpredictability, and information density within a document. A score of 0 means perfectly uniform and predictable; 100 means maximum chaos and information density.

Is my data secure? ▼

Yes. All uploads are encrypted in transit and at rest. We do not train models on your data. Enterprise plans include VPC isolation and custom data retention policies.

Can I cancel anytime? ▼

Absolutely. There are no contracts. Cancel directly from your billing dashboard and keep access until the end of your billing period.

Do you offer refunds? ▼

We offer a 14-day free trial so you can evaluate risk-free. If you are unhappy after upgrading, contact us within 7 days for a full refund.

What file types are supported? ▼

Explorer supports TXT and PDF. Paid plans add CSV, JSON, Markdown, DOCX, and direct API ingestion.
